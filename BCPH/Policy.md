# 🌲 BC Parks Reservation Assistant
## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 25, 2026

---

### 🛡️ Our Commitment to Privacy
The **BC Parks Reservation Assistant** extension (the "Extension") was built with a "Privacy First" approach. We believe that your data should belong to you, and our tool is designed to work entirely within your browser.

---

### 1. 📂 Data Collection and Usage
We strictly follow the principle of **Zero Data Collection**:
* **No Tracking:** We do not track your browsing history or search queries.
* **No Remote Storage:** No personal information, campsite preferences, or account details are ever sent to our servers or any third parties.
* **No Analytics:** We do not use any tracking pixels or analytical scripts.

### 2. 💻 Local Processing
All actions performed by the Extension happen **locally** on your device:
* **Form Auto-fill:** Your booking details (dates, guest count, vehicle info) are processed in real-time to assist with the BC Parks reservation forms.
* **Local Storage:** If you choose to save your preferences for future bookings, they are stored securely using the `chrome.storage` API on your own computer.

### 3. 🔑 Permissions Justification
To function correctly, the Extension requires the following permissions:
* **`storage`**: Used exclusively to remember your form preferences locally so you don't have to re-enter them.
* **`activeTab` / `scripting`**: Necessary to detect when you are on the official BC Parks website and to display the assistant interface.

### 4. ⚖️ Compliance
This Extension is not affiliated with, maintained, authorized, or sponsored by BC Parks or the Government of British Columbia. It is an independent tool created to improve the user experience of the reservation system.
